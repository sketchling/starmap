Star catalogue attribution
---------------------------
The file stars.csv shipped with this addon is a derived subset (brightest
3000 stars: name, J2000 RA/Dec, V magnitude, B-V colour, distance) of the
HYG Database v4.2, a compilation of the Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star, and
Gliese catalogues.
